Mira Klein on piano, silence and the shape of a set

10th July 2026

Mira Klein in rehearsal. Photo placeholder.

Pianist Mira Klein approaches music as architecture: sound, space, tension, release and the emotional logic that holds a set together.

JazzingFfm: How do you build the shape of a concert?

Mira Klein: I think about energy first. Not every piece needs to impress immediately. Some pieces are doors. Some are windows. Some are there to change the temperature of the room.

JazzingFfm: What role does silence play in your music?

Mira Klein: Silence is not empty. It is where the listener completes the phrase. I try to leave enough space so the music can breathe.

Mira Klein

Mira Klein is a pianist whose work explores sound, silence and the architecture of a concert set. Her music connects harmonic detail with a strong sense of space and dramatic shape.
