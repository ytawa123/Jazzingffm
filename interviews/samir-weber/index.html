Samir Weber on bass lines, patience and city rhythm

17th July 2026

Samir Weber with double bass. Photo placeholder.

Bassist Samir Weber thinks of the bass as a social instrument. In his playing, time is not only counted - it is shared, negotiated and shaped by the musicians around him.

JazzingFfm: What is the bass responsible for in a band?

Samir Weber: The bass gives direction without needing to stand in front. It can hold the music together, but it can also suggest where the music might go next. That balance is what I love.

JazzingFfm: Does the city affect your sense of rhythm?

Samir Weber: Definitely. Frankfurt has a very particular pace. Trains, rivers, small clubs, people moving through different scenes - all of that becomes part of how you feel time.

Samir Weber

Samir Weber is a double bassist interested in groove, musical responsibility and the social role of the bass. His playing treats time as something shared and shaped by the whole ensemble.
